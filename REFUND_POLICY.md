# GoGather Refund Policy

At GoGather, we aim to provide a fair and professional ticketing experience. Our refund policy is designed to balance user flexibility with event organizer commitments.

## Refund Tiers

| Cancellation Timing | Refund Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Within 2 Hours of Booking** | **100%** | Grace Period (applies if event is >24h away) |
| **> 48 Hours before Event** | **90%** | Standard Cancellation |
| **24 - 48 Hours before Event** | **50%** | Late Cancellation |
| **< 24 Hours before Event** | **0%** | Non-refundable |

## Important Details
1. **Processing Time**: Refunds typically reflect in your original payment method within **5-7 business days**.
2. **First-Timer Discounts**: If a first-timer discount was applied, the refund is calculated based on the *actually paid amount*.
3. **Event Cancellations**: If an event is cancelled by the organizer, you will receive a **100% refund** automatically.
4. **Non-Refundable Window**: Cancellations made within 24 hours of the event start time are strictly non-refundable to protect organizer logistics.

## How to Cancel
1. Log in to your GoGather account.
2. Navigate to **My Adventures**.
3. Locate your booking and click **Cancel Ticket**.
4. Review the calculated refund and confirm.

For any further queries, please reach out to our support team at [gogatherticketbooking@gmail.com](mailto:gogatherticketbooking@gmail.com).
